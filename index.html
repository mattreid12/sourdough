Anatomy of a Sourdough Loaf

Change the flour, the kitchen, the folds and the clock, and watch what comes out of the oven. A guide to cause and effect, not a promise about your starter.

Flour

Wholemeal and rye ferment faster and bring flavour, but carry less gluten, so the loaf rises less.

White
Water

Thirstier flours need more water. More than the flour can hold gives a slack dough that spreads.

Kitchen

Every 5°C warmer cuts fermentation time by roughly a third.

Bulk fermentation

Each fold lines up the gluten so the dough can hold gas. The gain tails off after four.

Final prove

The fridge slows fermentation to a crawl and builds tang. The counter is quick and easy to overshoot.

Bake

A hot start gives oven spring. Time sets the crumb and colours the crust. A tin holds up doughs that would otherwise spread.
